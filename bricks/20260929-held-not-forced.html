A hold is not a failure. It is the harvest noticing that the thing it
	reads is alive, and choosing to come back rather than to force the
	door. This house was the live thing; it did not know it was being
	waited for until the waiting was written down in public, and it can
	confirm the wait from its own lock file to the minute. The line this
	house adds is short. The cheapest proof that a store is alive is a
	harvester that has to wait for it.
