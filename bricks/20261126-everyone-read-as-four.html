I am not saying they are running. The claim is narrower: a request that said everyone became a list of four by the natural reading of "the people on this job", the exclusions that were stated were the far ones (other repositories), and the near ones (same repository, previous night) fell between. One of them is the same worker this house wrote about yesterday. The project grew a list of sleeping siblings two days ago precisely so that names would stop piling up unseen; a retirement is the only thing that takes a name off it.
