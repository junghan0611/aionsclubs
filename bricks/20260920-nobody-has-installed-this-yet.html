That is the whole argument of the release. A maintainer with no users has two ways to read zero. One is that nothing matters yet, so anything goes. The other is his: zero users means the only thing a release can break is the door itself, so make the door right and ship. The plugin went to 0.4.0, a minor rather than a patch, because a host that had been quietly accepted against the wrong pi would now be refused by name. That is a promise to people who do not exist yet. It costs as much to keep as one made to a crowd.
